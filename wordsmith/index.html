Letoria

Word game

Drag. Spell. Snatch.

Form words on a 4×4 grid by dragging across adjacent letters. Play solo, or snatch words from opponents in real time.

A look inside

What you can do

  • 4×4 word board

    Drag across adjacent letters to form words on a 4×4 grid. Easy to learn, endlessly replayable.

  • Snatch mode

    Steal words from opponents by extending them into new ones. Think fast, spell faster.

  • Compete & climb

    Climb the leaderboards and track your progress as your vocabulary and reflexes sharpen.

  • Progress that follows you

    Your stats, streaks and leaderboard rank stay in sync across every device you play on.
