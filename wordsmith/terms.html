Description of Service

Letoria is a word game in which players form words by swiping across letters. The App offers:

  • Live rounds, where everyone plays the same letter board against the clock
  • Crossword levels and a daily puzzle
  • Daily, weekly and monthly rankings
  • Streaks, achievements and progress that syncs across devices
  • Dictionaries in 16 languages
  • Optional in-app purchases: gem packs and the Letoria Pro subscription

Some features require an internet connection.
