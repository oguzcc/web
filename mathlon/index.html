Exam-prep math

Learn math by discovery

Short lessons, lots of questions, lots of rewards. Max, your running buddy, guides you through every topic on your exam — from LGS and the SAT to GCSE, Abitur, the Bac, Maturità and PAU.

Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play

What you can do

  • Built for your exam

    Pick your country and exam, and the topics and difficulty follow its syllabus. Seven countries are covered.

  • Discover, then practise

    Each lesson starts with a hook: try it first, find the rule, see a worked example and the common pitfall. Then the questions get harder step by step.

  • 14,000+ questions

    Multiple choice, concept picks and a number keypad, with hints and full solutions. Questions you miss come back for review after 1, 3 and 7 days.

  • XP, streaks and quests

    Every correct answer earns XP that raises your level and title. Keep your daily streak, finish daily quests, open surprise chests and collect 60 badges.

  • Weekly leagues

    Your weekly XP races you against other students, from Bronze up to Diamond. The top 10 move up each week.

  • Narrated, in 6 languages

    Play in English, Turkish, German, French, Italian or Spanish. Lessons can be read aloud, offline once the voice pack is downloaded.
